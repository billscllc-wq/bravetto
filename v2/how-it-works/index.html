How it works

From a work question to a useful answer.

A person with a question

Someone trying to understand or move work forward—about a record, a decision, or the status of something.

Information in your systems

The answer may sit in one system or several: ERP, CRM, inventory, finance, help desk, notes.

An answer in context

Abë brings the pieces together so the answer makes sense for the work at hand. Access and consent shape what she can use.

Three steps

No training required.

  1. You ask.

    One real work question, the way you’d ask a colleague. No system names, no special prompts.

  2. Abë finds it.

    She draws on the systems you’ve connected, within the access your organization sets.

  3. You get an answer.

    One you can act on. Ask a follow-up without starting over.

Working together

Start with one workflow, one team.

Timing and scope depend on the work, the systems, and the access involved. Here’s the usual shape.

  1. A discovery call

    30 minutes. We learn how your work runs, which question keeps coming up, and where the answers live.

  2. A custom demo

    If it looks like a fit, a second meeting may follow, with a demo shaped around your work.

  3. One workflow

    Together we pick the one workflow where better answers matter most.

  4. Connect and measure

    We agree on the measure with you before we start, then connect it to your systems and run it with your people.
