Trust and security

Security and data handling, in plain words.

Your IT and security reviewers will have specific questions. Here’s where we stand, and how to get the details for your setup.

  • Works with the systems you already run.

    No rip-and-replace. AbëONE connects to the systems your teams use today; SAP is one example.

  • Built with governance and consent in mind.

    Access and consent shape what information AbëONE can use. We’ll go through how that works for your organization.

  • We’ll walk you through security and data handling in a conversation.

    Details depend on your systems and deployment, so we cover them with your team directly.

Bring your questions

What reviewers usually ask.

We’d rather answer these with your setup in front of us than publish a general version that doesn’t fit. Bring them to the conversation:

Ask about security and data handling
  • Where does our data live, and does it move?
  • Which AI providers and models are involved?
  • What is stored, for how long, and who can delete it?
  • Who sets permissions, and how?
  • Is any of our data used to train models?
  • Which certifications and standards apply?
