Illustration—how an answer is meant to work

The question

“Show me last quarter’s margins by region.”

What Abë does

Checks which regions this person is cleared to see, then draws on the connected finance system for those regions only.

What you get

Margins for the regions you’re cleared to see, in plain words, ready for a follow-up question.

The boundary

For the other regions, Abë says she can’t share them—and tells you who can approve access.
